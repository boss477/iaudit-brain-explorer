How to read this study

Recorded predictions

Values come from recorded project runs. Selecting a case replays those predictions; no model runs in your browser. The image demo includes 26 selected cases; the full CSV includes all 799 scan records.

Frozen CNN features, ridge heads

A ridge head is a small linear model using features from a frozen CNN. These heads were trained on IXI Guys+HH only. IXI predictions are out-of-fold; SALD was not used to train the heads.

Training overlap

BrainAgeNeXt’s training included IXI through openBHB and eNKI through NKI-RS. SALD is the independent comparison. Tabular models only appear for IXI, where tissue-volume features were extracted.

The images and the 3D model

Case MRI images are the research repository’s skull-stripped, bias-corrected MNI152-aligned slices in neurological view. The shared GLB is the supplied anatomical reference. Its geometry does not change with the case or the model’s predicted age.

What’s included

The table shows all 26 image cases, including ages, sex, site and every available prediction. The 799-scan CSV includes the full cohort ages, recorded ethnicity where available, training overlap, model outputs and reliability values. Case JSON also includes selection notes, BrainAgeNeXt’s five internal predictions, 234 MRI image paths and cohort summaries. Empty CSV cells mean that model was not run on that scan. Original MRI volumes are not included.
